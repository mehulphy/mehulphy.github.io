---
title: "We Were Scalars"
lang: en
order: 1
featured: true
note: "Written for my teacher when I was in college, and dedicated to every teacher."
---

We were scalars,
you made us vectors,
Our motion was unaccelerated,
Your constant guiding force, made it accelerated.

In your classroom, the world came to life,
Each lesson a beacon in our educational strife,
You painted the canvas of wisdom with care,
Filling our hearts and minds with knowledge so rare.

With patience as vast as the cosmic expanse,
You answered our questions, gave us a chance,
To explore the realms where knowledge abounds,
You ignited the spark, dispelling our doubts.

Like stars that light up the darkest night,
Your wisdom illuminated our paths so bright,
In the galaxy of learning, you were our North Star,
Guiding us onward, no matter how near or far.

You sculpted characters, instilled virtues so pure,
Taught us compassion, integrity, and much more,
With your dedication and love, you planted the seed,
Of a lifelong quest for knowledge, indeed.

On this day, we honor you with heartfelt grace,
For your influence transcends time and space,
Teachers, you're the architects of our dreams,
In the symphony of life, you're the guiding themes.
