Welcome.

I’m Mehul, a learner and an educator. For more than twenty years I have worked in education: teaching higher secondary physics, studying pedagogy in real classrooms, and designing inquiry-based science and K-12 computer science programmes that have reached thousands of students and teachers across India. What drives it all is how people learn, through productive struggle, honest questions, and reasoning from evidence.

This is my personal corner of the web, where I share my reflections and my poetry. To know more about me, read the about page or my CV.
