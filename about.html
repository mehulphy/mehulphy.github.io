About

Hello, I’m Mehul.

I’m a learner, first and always. A thirst to understand nature led me to study physics, and the joy of sharing what I learn led me to teaching. For more than twenty years since, I have been an educator, teaching higher-secondary physics, and designing how science, and lately computer science, is taught in classrooms across India and beyond.

What holds it all together is an interest in how people learn: productive struggle, student questioning, and reasoning from evidence. Today I’m Chief Learning Officer at Qubits Learning, and I host Intellectual Rigour, long-form conversations with scientists and mathematicians.

This blog is where I think out loud. The poems are where I stop thinking and just listen.

A motto in three words

Learn.Educate.Enlighten.
